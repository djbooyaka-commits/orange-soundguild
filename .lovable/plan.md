# How Plan mode works

Nothing on your site changes in this plan.

1. You ask for something.
2. I look through your site and write a step-by-step plan.
3. You choose what happens next:
   - **Approve:** I switch to building and make the changes.
   - **Skip:** nothing gets built.
   - **Ask for changes:** I update the plan and show it to you again.

Using Plan mode costs much less than building. Credits for the work are only used once you approve.
